# Two Lines to Your Past Self

A two-line warning sent back to the writer's past self: the first line warns against saying "I'm fine" to Maya at the kitchen table on a Sunday night, and the second line traces what that small lie cost — her leaving believing he didn't care, and a year spent trying to take it back. The entry is a single concrete moment, a sentence actually spoken, with a clear cause and effect, written plainly to one specific person.
